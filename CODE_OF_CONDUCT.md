# Code of Conduct

BetterXen welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Discussions should be about the technical work and conducted with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, constructive feedback supported by evidence.
- Respect disagreements, boundaries, privacy and differing levels of experience.
- Credit other contributors and upstream authors accurately.
- Accept corrections and disclose uncertainty rather than presenting speculation as fact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation, doxxing, sharing private information without consent, intimidation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Neither are personal attacks on contributors, competing projects or their communities.

## Reporting and moderation

If possible, use an available private contact channel for a repository maintainer or GitHub's reporting mechanisms. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an issue. If a maintainer is involved in the concern, use GitHub's reporting tools rather than contacting that maintainer directly.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation or block accounts when necessary to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect the privacy of affected people. Do not retaliate against anyone reporting a concern in good faith.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces officially managed by BetterXen.
